# NeverQuest first test session

About 75 minutes. Do the steps in order; skip any that don't apply to where you are.

## Before you start

1. Install **BugSack** and **BugGrabber** (they catch and show errors).
2. Replace your `NeverQuest` folder with the new one, then log in.

## The checklist

1. **Login.** Chat says "NeverQuest 1.11.4 by NeverHeal84 loaded". Stand somewhere outdoors and run `/nq check`. Copy the window text (Ctrl+C) and save it to send later.
2. **Panels.** `/nq` opens the window and all six tabs switch. The XP panel and arrow show up. Drag them where you like, then `/nq lock`.
3. **Options.** Esc > Options > AddOns > NeverQuest. All six pages open (NeverQuest, Guide & Routes, Tracker, Display & Maps, Spawns & Objectives, Scale & Text) and scroll if they're long. Flip a couple of settings and back. On Scale & Text, try a bigger text size and a different font: the main window, tracker, XP panel and arrow should all change.
4. **Quests and Nearby tabs.** Your quest log shows XP and distances. Nearby lists quests to pick up (open the world map once if it's empty).
5. **Guide and arrow.** The Guide tab's top step matches where the arrow points. Walk toward it: the distance drops and the arrow turns green when you face it. **Check the arrow isn't mirrored** (it should point left when the target is on your left).
6. **Quest NPCs.** Talking to a quest giver auto-accepts; holding Shift skips that. Finished quests turn in by themselves. With several reward choices, chat suggests the highest vendor value.
7. **Waypoints.** `/way 50 50 Test`: the arrow, a minimap pin and a world map pin appear. Walk there and chat says "arrived". Alt+click the world map to add one. Ctrl+click (Blizzard's pin) and the arrow follows it too.
8. **Routes.** Target something inside a cave or building, or in another zone. The arrow should send you to the entrance or exit first and show the game's directions, and the Guide tab shows "via:". If you have two or more flight points and pick a far target, it should say "Fly from ... to ...".
9. **Minimap pins.** Quest givers and turn-ins appear at the right spots. If you use a rotating minimap (Blizzard setting), check that the pins rotate with it.
10. **World map.** You see numbered guide steps joined by a line from where you stand. Turn off "Lines joining the route steps" (Display & Maps > World map): the line goes, the numbers stay.
11. **Quest item button.** On a quest with a usable item, a button appears and works. Bind it under Options > Keybindings > AddOns > NeverQuest.
12. **Target button.** It's off by default now: the quest item bar shows no TARGET pill. Turn it on with `/nq buttons target`; on a kill quest it shows the mob's name and clicking it targets one nearby. With the pill off, the "Target next NPC or mob" key binding still targets.
13. **Combat.** Fight with the item button showing: no errors, and the buttons update after the fight. If you die, release: the arrow should point to your corpse, and switch back to your route after you resurrect.
14. **Recorder.** `/nq record Test`, do two or three quests, visit a vendor, `/nq note hello`, then `/nq stop`. The Guides tab lists it and Export shows text.
15. **Journal.** Turn-ins show under Journal > History. After a level-up, Splits shows the level time.
16. **1-60 plan.** `/nq plan` shows your race and class route (Optimized 1-60 by default) with your current leg marked. Click the style button at the top right to switch to Zone by zone and back. Try the Race and Class buttons, then Mine. In `/nq check`, the "Leveling plan" line should find all (or nearly all) zones. At an even level, the guide shows a trainer reminder that clears when you visit your trainer.
17. **Spawns and objectives.** Turn on enemy nameplates. On a kill quest, kill two or three mobs, then open the world map: colored dots appear where they died, and hovering one names the mob and quest. On a drop quest, loot a quest item and check the dot names the mob that dropped it. Walk near other quest mobs out of combat and dots should appear for them too. The guide step should say "known spot", and once you reach a spot the arrow moves on to the next one.
18. **Marks.** Alt+right-click the world map: the menu lists your target (if any), your open objectives, a typed name and a note. Make one of each kind you like, then right-click a dot and pick "Forget this spot", and Shift+click a mark to remove it. Try `/nq mark` with a mob targeted. In `/nq check`, the "Spawn learning" line should say "all game functions present".
19. **Route planner.** With four or more quests in one zone, the Guide tab's steps should follow one route (numbered, with "Then turn in" steps and a "Route:" summary row), and `/nq route` should list it. Turn-ins near an objective should come right before or after it, not after a trip across the zone. Far, low-XP quests show as "later".
20. **Quest chains.** Turn in a quest whose NPC immediately offers the next one: the next quest's step or tracker line should say "chain" or "follow-up". In `/nq check`, the "Quest chains" line shows how many of your quests are in a game quest line.
21. **Tracker.** The NeverQuest tracker is on the right, grouped by zone (current zone first) and numbered in guide order. Blizzard's quest list should be empty (its bonus objectives and other sections stay). Click a zone header, right-click the tracker header and a quest, and drag the header to move it. Try "Sort by priority" and "Leave it alone" for Blizzard's list on the Tracker page: Leave should give your tracked quests back.
22. **Quest givers.** Open the world map in your zone: quest givers show "!" with a colored level number, and gray or red quests aren't there. Open a neighboring zone: its quest givers appear after a moment. Turn on "Include gray" and "Include red" under Display & Maps > Quest givers and check they appear. Click a quest giver for a waypoint. In `/nq check`, the "Quest givers on maps" line counts them by color.
23. **Leveling styles.** On Optimized, level past your current leg's window while still in that zone: a "Time to move on: head to ..." step should appear after any turn-ins next to you, and the arrow should go there. On Zone by zone, the guide should keep you in your zone until its quests are done, then show "Zone done: head to ...". In `/nq check`, the "Leveling style" line says where it wants you.
24. **Minimap button.** The NeverQuest button sits on the minimap's edge. Click it (window opens), Shift+click (tracker toggles), right-click (quick settings), and drag it around the edge. `/reload` keeps its spot.
25. **Party (optional).** With a friend who also runs NeverQuest, the Quests tab shows their progress on shared quests, and a spot one of you learns or marks shows up for the other. With party catch-up on: the guide shows a "Party catch-up" row, quests you share come first, a quest you've finished that your friend hasn't shows "waiting for ..." on its turn-in (and a "Help ..." step if a spot is known), "Share with your party" rows share when clicked, and a "Regroup" step appears when you're far apart.

26. **Raid attunements.** Open the Raids tab (`/nq raids`): your faction's Onyxia's Lair chain, The Barrow Deeps and Hyjal Summit (no attunement). Click "Show Horde/Alliance" to browse the other one. Right-click a step to mark it done, again to undo. Click the raid row: the footer says "Following", and the guide shows the chain's next step (second while leveling, first at 60). If you're on a chain quest already, that step says "on it" and everything before it is done. In `/nq check`, the "Raid attunements" line shows your progress.
27. **Party announcements.** In a party, `/nq announce test` posts "NeverQuest: announcements are working." in party chat. Finish one objective of a quest with two or more objectives (party chat shows "objective done"), then finish the quest ("complete, ready to turn in"). Turn on "Quests turned in" under Options > NeverQuest > Party announcements and hand something in. `/nq announce off` should stop all of it. In `/nq check`, the "Party announcements" line says where messages go.

28. **Map route.** With a few unfinished quests, open the world map: the numbered route and its line go to quest objectives (and finished turn-ins and pickups), not to the NPCs where unfinished quests get handed in. Turn on "Include turn-ins for unfinished quests" under Display & Maps > World map and the "Then turn in" stops join the line. If a step says "the map only marks the quest NPC", tell me which quest.
29. **Setup wizard.** `/nq setup` opens the wizard with the NeverQuest logo and "by NeverHeal84" at the top. Step through every page with Next and Back, change a setting or two (they should take effect straight away), and Finish. "Use recommended" on the first page jumps to the last page. On a fresh install (no NeverQuest settings saved yet) it opens by itself a few seconds after login; an existing install just gets a one-time chat note about it.
30. **Help.** `/nq help` opens the help window; click through the topics on the left and scroll a long one. `/nq help raids` opens the raid topic, `/nq commands` lists commands in chat, and the Help button in the main window and the minimap menu open it too. The main window title and the minimap button show the logo.

31. **Auto loot.** Kill something and loot it: the window empties at once. Hold Shift while looting the next one: the window stays open. In a group with roll items, those are left for the roll. `/nq loot off` turns it off.
32. **Sell junk.** With a few gray items in your bags, open a vendor: they sell and chat says "sold N junk items for ...". Check the Buyback tab. Open a vendor again holding Shift with junk in your bags: nothing sells. Away from a vendor, `/nq junk` says what your junk is worth. In `/nq check`, the "Sell junk" line says which way it sells on your client.

33. **Button bar.** With a quest that needs a mob killed, the bar near the bottom of the screen shows the NeverQuest logo on the left and a "TARGET" pill with the mob's name (no floating name above it). Click the pill to target the mob; bind "Target next NPC or mob" and the key shows on the pill. Quest item buttons are rounded with a gold frame. Drag the logo to move the bar. Send a screenshot if anything looks off.

34. **"...and that note you found".** With it in your log (Alliance), the guide's turn-in step says "at the Burned-Out Remains, The Barrens" with directions and "Cozy Sleeping Bag 1/6"; the arrow and world map point to the ruined tower south of Camp Taurajo, and the tracker lists it under The Barrens. Each later step (Stepping Stones, Scramble, Wet Job, Eagle's Fist, This Must Be The Place) should point at its spot too. Tell me if any spot is off.

35. **Hand-ins in another zone.** Finish a quest that's handed in somewhere other than where you got it (like the Stonetalon/Barrens one in your screenshot). The world map's numbered "?" and the guide step should point at the real hand-in, or say "somewhere in <zone>" / "where they are isn't known yet"; never back at the pickup spot. If one still points at the pickup spot, send the quest name and what its quest log text says.

36. **Shared and remote quests.** Accept a quest a party member shares, and hand in a quest through a pop-up (if you find one): neither should leave a "?" at the spot where you were standing. Click a finished quest in the tracker: the arrow goes to its hand-in, with the NPC's name when NeverQuest knows it.

37. **Reward upgrades.** Hand in a quest with a choice of rewards. A green arrow marks one and chat says why ("... is an upgrade over [your item] (+3 Agility, ...)" or "no upgrade here; highest vendor value: ..."). Check the pick makes sense for your class; send the quest name and rewards if it doesn't. Under Options > NeverQuest, "Take upgrades, else value" should take it for you.

38. **Repairs.** With damaged gear, open a vendor that repairs (a blacksmith or armorer): chat says "repaired everything for ...". Open one holding Shift: nothing happens. In a guild that pays repairs, `/nq repair guild` and repair again: chat says "(guild funds)".

39. **Reminders.** Fill your quest log to the limit: a guide row says it's full and names quests to drop, and talking to a quest giver says it in chat. Drop to under 10 food and water: a row says so. With 3 or fewer free bag slots: a row says so and names quest items you can delete. Start a logout in the open world: chat reminds you about rested XP.

40. **Flight paths and hearth.** In a zone with a flight master you haven't met, a "Get the flight path" step appears (near the top when you're close); click it and the arrow points there. Bind your hearthstone at an inn, quest somewhere far away, and finish a quest handed in near that inn: once nothing is left near you and your hearthstone is ready, the guide's first step says to hearth back and the hearthstone shows on the quest item bar.

41. **Quest database.** About a minute after logging in, `/nq check` shows "Quest database: N quests known" (and "scanning" while it works). `/nq db` lists your zone; `/nq db <part of a quest name>` finds it; `/nq db way <id>` points the arrow. Open the world map on a zone a few levels above you: faded "!" markers with levels show quests that open later. On an alt, quests your main has seen show up there too.

42. **Session and group.** `/nq session` shows this session's XP, quests and gold. Ctrl-click an elite quest in the guide: an LFG message with the quest link appears in your chat box (nothing is sent until you press Enter).

43. **1.13 checks.** Right-click a reminder row in the guide (for example "Bags almost full"): it disappears until your next login. Learn a new flight path: its "Get the flight path" step goes away at once. After updating, chat says "updated to 1.13.0. /nq news shows what's new" once, and `/nq news` opens that page. During the first minutes after login (while the quest database scans), the guide and tracker should not flicker or stutter; say so if they do.

44. **Finished quests you have levelled past (1.13.2).** Carry a few completed quests whose turn-in is in a zone you have left, far enough that they have gone gray in your log, and leave "Include gray quests" off. `/nq route` should list every one of them, the guide should show the trip, and the tracker should agree. One gray hand-in on its own, with nothing else near it, should stay off the route and be listed as "later" instead. An unfinished gray quest should still be hidden while that option is off.

45. **Drops window (1.14.0).** With a couple of "collect N of these" quests in your log, run `/nq drops`. Every item you're short of should be listed with its count, and a kill-only quest should not be. Kill something that drops one: its name should appear on the gray line under that item, with a distance. Click the item to point the arrow at it, Shift+click to link it. Right-click the header to change the sorting and to turn the source line off. Check the key binding (Open or close the drops window) under Esc > Options > Keybindings > AddOns > NeverQuest, and that the window stays where you drag it after a reload.

46. **Arrow quality (1.14.0).** Watch the waypoint arrow while you turn on the spot. The edges should stay clean rather than crawling or stepping, and the outline should stay dark while the fill goes green, yellow or orange. Try each of the four sizes under Options > Display & Maps: the largest should be just as sharp as the smallest, and the title line should sit right under the arrow at every size, not float below a gap.

47. **Nameplate icons and tooltips (1.14.0).** Turn enemy nameplates on (V). Mobs for a kill quest should show a sword, the mob the guide's top step names should show a skull, and each should have your count under it; a mob no quest wants should show nothing. Loot a quest item from a mob, then look at another of the same kind: it should now show a bag, and its tooltip should name the quest and your count. Finish the objective and the icons should go. Hover a quest item in your bags for its "Quest:" line. In a party with another NeverQuest user, mob tooltips should show their progress. Try `/nq plates off`.
48. **Bag finds, sharing, flights (1.14.0).** Put a piece of gear better than what you're wearing in your bags: the guide should add an "Upgrade in your bags" row. Fill your bags with some gray items in them: the bags row should name the cheapest. Turn on "Share quests with my party as I pick them up" and accept a quest in a party: the others should be offered it. Take a flight the guide planned twice: the second time the arrow should count down.
49. **Other languages (1.14.0).** On a non-English client, a kill quest should still give the TARGET button a mob name, and `/nq check` should report the Language line as OK.

50. **Class tab (1.15.0).** `/nq class` should open a seventh tab that still fits the window. The checklist should be for your class, rows at or below your level should say "now", and clicking one should tick it to "done" and back. With a class quest in your log it should appear at the top, and clicking it should point the arrow.

51. **Profession quests and the options (1.15.0).** In the Class tab, the Professions button should list your own professions with your skill beside each, and rows should say "now" only when you have both the skill and the level. Under Options > Guide & Routes, turning "Profession quests section" off should leave the tab with class quests only; turning "Class quests section" off instead should rename the tab to Trades; turning both off should remove the tab and close the gap.

52. **Finished quests and travel (1.16.0).** Complete a few quests whose hand-in spot NeverQuest hasn't learned (pick them up before installing, or use `/nq forget`), then stand in their zone with work waiting elsewhere. The guide should open with "N finished quests waiting in this zone" and list them above anything that takes you out of the zone, not under "later".
53. **Profession cap (1.16.0).** Get a profession within ten points of its rank cap (75, 150 or 225). The guide should say "Nearly capped" with the numbers, and the wording should change to "You're in a town" when you're in one. A profession at 300 should never be mentioned.

54. **Spawn dots (1.17.0).** Hover a learned spawn dot: the first line should say what it is and its level. Kill some low mobs in a starting zone, then come back at a much higher level with "Hide spots well below my level" on: those dots should be gone, while anything a current quest points at stays. Turning the option off should bring them all back.

55. **Review release (1.20.0), the parts only the game can confirm.**
    - *Guild answers.* With two NeverQuest users in a guild, one runs `/nq db ask`. The asker should see one "N quest starts from your guild" line a minute or so later, and N should match what the answerer had to give (up to 120). Anyone else in the guild should see nothing in chat.
    - *Invites.* With `/nq invites known`, an invite from a guildmate, a character friend and a Battle.net friend on another realm should each be accepted; one from a stranger should be left on screen.
    - *Dismissing the arrow.* Right-click the arrow while it follows a guide step. It should move to the next step, not go blank, and clicking the dismissed step in the guide should bring it back.
    - *Tracker in a dungeon.* With Blizzard's list hidden, walk into a dungeon: Blizzard's tracker should appear with the boss list, and go again when you leave.
    - *Tracker at logout.* With Blizzard's list hidden, log out, disable NeverQuest at the character screen and log in: your quests should be tracked in Blizzard's list, and accepting a quest should track it automatically. Re-enable NeverQuest and it should all be hidden again within a second of logging in.
    - *Standing still.* `/nq debug`, then stand still for a minute: there should be no stream of guide or route lines. Walk a few steps and they resume.
    - *Talk-to quests.* Accept a quest whose only step is "speak to someone": no "Ready to turn in" alert and no party announcement.
56. **QuestieDB or your own, and wrong places (1.21.6, QuestieDB from 1.22.0).**
    - *The choice.* `/nq setup`, go to the Quest database page and pick **Build my own**: the world map should lose its faded "Known quest" markers for quests nobody with NeverQuest has seen, and `/nq lib` should say QuestieDB is not in use. Pick **QuestieDB** again and they come back within a few seconds.
    - *Not here.* Find a faded known quest marker you can't actually pick up (one you've done, or one with nobody there). Stand on it for about ten seconds: it should disappear from the world map, and `/nq db fixes` should list it as "the giver wasn't there for you".
    - *Ctrl+click.* Ctrl+click a known quest marker on the world map: chat says it won't be shown there any more and it disappears. `/nq db fixes undo <its number>` brings it back.
    - *Moved.* Take or hand in a quest at an NPC whose marker was somewhere else: `/nq db fixes` should list it as "found somewhere else", with where.
    - *Export.* `/nq db export` should mention the places where QuestieDB's data was wrong.
57. **Dungeon quests (1.21.7).**
    - *The wizard.* `/nq setup`: there should be a Dungeon quests page with the switch and a tick box for each dungeon on your side.
    - *Before the Deadmines (Alliance).* At level 16 or 17 with Westfall open, the guide should put Deadmines pickups near the top: "Oh Brother...", "Collecting Memories" and "Underground Assault" in Stormwind, and The Defias Brotherhood from Gryan Stoutmantle, labelled "pre-quest for Red Silk Bandanas (The Deadmines)". A row "The Deadmines quests: ..." should count them.
    - *Cherry-picking.* `/nq dungeons deadmines` (or untick it in Choose dungeons): those pickups should leave the route. Tick it again and they come back.
    - *Waiting.* With a Deadmines quest in your log, the route shouldn't send you to the dungeon's entrance; its step says "done in The Deadmines". Inside the Deadmines it's routed normally.
    - *New Forever dungeons.* Pick up a quest for The Hall of Thanes or another new dungeon on one character; `/nq dungeons` on an alt should count it.

58. **QuestieDB (1.22.0).**
    - *Installed with NeverQuest.* Install or update through the CurseForge app: QuestieDB should be installed along with NeverQuest. In game, `/nq lib` should say "QuestieDB 1.x.x in use" with a count of quests placed, and the world map should show faded known quests in a zone you haven't played yet.
    - *One quest.* `/nq lib 783` on an Alliance character: Deputy Willem should be the start and Marshal McBride the finish, both in Elwynn Forest.
    - *Build my own.* Choose **Build my own** in `/nq setup`: `/nq lib` says QuestieDB isn't in use, and markers for quests nobody with NeverQuest has seen go away. Choose **QuestieDB** again.
    - *Without QuestieDB.* Disable QuestieDB on the character screen's AddOns list and log in: one chat line should say QuestieDB isn't installed, `/nq check` should show a WARN line for QuestieDB, and there should be no Lua errors. Turn it back on.
    - *Old files.* After a CurseForge update the NeverQuest folder should have no `Libs` folder, no `Data\Questie` and no `Data\Lookups.lua`. A manual update over the old folder can leave them behind; that's harmless, since nothing loads them.
    - *Questie as well.* With the Questie addon itself enabled, NeverQuest's giver and spawn pins should still step aside (`/nq questie`), as before.
59. **Reading QuestieDB for your character (1.22.1).**
    - *Login.* Log in with QuestieDB enabled: no stutter in the first few seconds, and within about ten seconds `/nq lib` shows the count of quests placed without "still loading".
    - *A quest you can't take.* Pick a faded marker and run `/nq lib <its number>`: the last line should say it is open to you now or opens at a level. Try a quest you know is for another class: it should say it's not for this character, and it should have no marker.
    - *Professions.* On a character without Cooking, there should be no marker for a cooking quest such as Clamlette Surprise in Gadgetzan. On one with the skill, there should be.
    - *New races.* On one of the new WoW Forever races, your starting zone and capital should show quest markers as on any other race.
    - *Dungeons.* `/nq dungeons` near level 28 should count Gnomeregan quests in the twenties, not two or three.
    - *The wizard.* `/nq setup`, Quest database page: a green line says QuestieDB is installed, with its version.
60. **Smarter zones, skipping and the missed list (1.23.0).**
    - *Zones by XP per hour.* At level 10 or above in the Optimized style, `/nq zones` should list zones that fit your level and side with an XP per hour, quest count and travel time. Your own zone should say "(you're here)", and zones on the other continent should say "by boat or zeppelin". No other race's starting zone and no zone you've outleveled should appear. The guide's "Time to move on" step should name the top zone, and the reason should mention XP/hour.
    - *Staying put.* Quest in a zone with plenty left: the guide shouldn't send you elsewhere unless `/nq zones` shows another zone far ahead (about twice yours).
    - *Skip.* Click Skip on a quest step: the step goes, the next one takes the arrow, and `/nq missed` lists the quest as skipped. In Quests tab > Missed, right-click it and choose Go back for it: the guide should open with "Go back for:" and the arrow should point there. `/nq missed unskip <id>` takes a skipped quest back.
    - *Drop.* Abandon a quest from the quest log: it should show on the missed list as dropped. Pick it up again: it should leave the list.
    - *Left behind.* Leave a zone with a quest still open there: in the next zone, the missed list should show it as left behind with its zone.
    - *Drop advice.* In the Optimized style with a gray or failed quest in your log, the guide should show a reminder once and `/nq drop` should list the quest with a reason. Right-click a quest in the guide or tracker: the menu should have Skip, Drop and Go back for it. A class quest or a dungeon quest should never be suggested.
    - *Zone by zone.* Switch to Zone by zone (`/nq style zone`): gray quests in your zone should show on the route, `/nq drop` should say this style doesn't suggest drops, and the guide should keep you in the zone until its quests are done. Untick "Zone by zone: finish every quest in a zone" under Options > Guide & Routes: gray quests should drop off the route.
61. **Dropdowns, resizing and quieter tooltips (1.24.0).**
    - *Dropdowns.* In the plan view (Guide tab > 1-60 plan), click the style, Race and Class buttons: each opens a menu, and nothing changes until you pick something. Do the same with Gray in the Nearby tab, the view button in the Quests tab, the faction button in the Raids tab, and a few settings in Options and the setup wizard.
    - *Main window.* Drag its bottom right corner: the list shows more rows as it grows. Close and reopen it, and log out and in: the size is kept. `/nq reset` puts it back.
    - *Tracker.* With ten or more quests, drag the tracker's bottom corner up to make it short: the quests scroll with the mouse wheel and a thin bar shows where you are. Click the "Quests" header to fold it: it shrinks up towards its top edge, not from the middle. Fold a zone too.
    - *Tracker right-click.* Right-click a quest: Share with party is greyed out when you're alone and works in a group, Abandon quest asks with the game's own box, and Link in chat and Show on the map work.
    - *Tooltips.* Hover the arrow: nothing shows. Hover a guide step: one line. Set Options > Display & Maps > Hover tooltips to Full: the old detail comes back. Set it to Off: nothing anywhere.
    - *Minimap.* With a kill quest in your log near its mobs, tiny dots show on the minimap where they've been seen. Rares and other spots don't.
62. **Zones that follow you (1.25.0).**
    - *Below 10.* On a new character, `/nq zones` should list zones with XP per hour from level 1, and the guide's reason should mention XP/hour rather than only your plan.
    - *Another starting zone.* Walk or fly to another race's starting zone on your continent (a human to Dun Morogh, an orc to Mulgore) and take a few quests: the guide should stay there and not send you home while it has quests for you.
    - *Across the sea.* Take the boat or zeppelin to the other continent (an Undead to the Barrens or Durotar): once you arrive, the guide should work from there, staying in that zone and picking the next zone on that continent.
    - *Zone by zone.* In the Zone by zone style, stand in a zone your plan doesn't name that has quests for you: the guide stays and works through them. When it's done, the "Zone done" step names the best zone from where you are.
63. **Party tracking (1.26.0).** Best with two or three people, at least one without NeverQuest.
    - *Party view.* Group up with a quest in common: the tracker should open with a Party section, a line per member with "N shared" and distance, and "no NeverQuest" for anyone without it. Click a member: the arrow points at them. Click the Party header: it folds to one line.
    - *Without NeverQuest.* Have the member without NeverQuest pick up one of your quests: within a few seconds their line should count it, and the quest's party line should say "<name> on it".
    - *Help.* Finish a quest a member running NeverQuest is still on: a "Help: <quest>" line should appear, and a party icon on the minimap where its mobs or objects are. Click either: the arrow points at the work.
    - *Alerts.* When they finish an objective of a quest you share, your chat should say "<name> finished <objective> (<quest>)." with a sound. When everyone has finished a quest, "Everyone's ready to hand in <quest>." once, and "Hand in together" in the tracker.
    - *Switches.* Right-click the tracker's Party header: untick "Party alerts in chat" and the next alert shouldn't show; tick it again. Try `/nq party alerts off`, `/nq party` (it should list what's on) and `/nq party alerts on`. The first alert ever should be followed by a gray line saying how to turn them off. Nothing should show in a raid.
64. **Mobs, drops and objects on the maps (1.26.0).** With QuestieDB installed and "QuestieDB" chosen in the setup wizard.
    - *Before you've been there.* Take a kill quest and a collect quest in a zone you haven't played this character in. Tiny coloured dots should show on the minimap near the mobs, and on the world map when zoomed in, without you having seen them yet. Hover one: it names the mob, says "Drops <item>" for the collect quest, the quest, and "From QuestieDB".
    - *Finished objectives.* Finish one objective of a quest with two: its dots go, the other's stay.
    - *Switch.* Right-click a QuestieDB dot on the world map: "Hide QuestieDB spots" should remove them; tick "Include QuestieDB's spots for my quests" under Options > Spawns & Objectives to bring them back.
    - *Known quests nearby.* Stand near quest givers you haven't talked to: faded "!" markers should show on the minimap (on by default now).

65. **Quest icon tooltips (1.26.1).** Hover a "!" quest giver on the world map: two lines, the quest's name in its difficulty colour and "Level N", nothing else. Hover a dungeon quest's giver (the Deadmines quests in Westfall, say): "Level N, Dungeon". An elite quest should say "Elite". Do the same on the minimap and for a quest you're on (its objective marker) and one ready to hand in. Set Options > Display & Maps > Hover tooltips to Full: quest icons should still show only those lines. Set it to Off: nothing.

66. **Route order and the map line (1.27.0).** Take four or five quests in a zone with a town in the middle (the Barrens from the Crossroads, or Westfall from Sentinel Hill), work spread around the zone and turn-ins back in town.
    - *The line.* Open the world map: the yellow line should go through every stop in order, with small unnumbered markers where unfinished quests will be handed in. It should never cut straight across the map between two numbered stops when the route goes back to town between them.
    - *The order.* Follow the guide: it shouldn't zigzag (north, then south, then back north) when there's an order that covers the same stops with less walking. `/nq route` lists every stop.
    - *While you're there.* With a long quest on the route, take a short quest whose mobs are right beside it: it should go on the route next to it, even if the route already runs past half an hour.
    - *Lines round the mountain (1.28.4).* Wetlands, heading for the hills above the Dragonmaw camp (53, 58) from the south or west: a second or two after the route appears, the world map and minimap lines should bend round the high ground, not run up it. Check the same in Stonetalon, Thousand Needles and anywhere a line crossed a cliff before. `/nq paths line` makes them straight; `/nq paths` reports both switches. Watch for a hitch when first entering the Barrens (the biggest zone's data is unpacked then).
    - *Minimap route line (1.28.3).* With a route showing, a gold line should run on the minimap from your arrow to the next stop and on towards the following ones, ending at the minimap's edge, and move smoothly as you walk and turn (try a rotating minimap and zooming the minimap in and out). `/nq mmline` and the checkbox under Minimap should remove it and bring it back.
    - *Dungeon quests and the back-and-forth (1.28.2).* With an unfinished dungeon quest whose marker is its turn-in NPC (Blueleaf Tubers at Ratchet) and nothing else to do nearby: the arrow must not point at the NPC, and the guide lists the quest under "later" with "done inside the dungeon". `/nq paths` should say walking paths are off after updating; stand between two stops for a minute and the arrow should not swap between them.
    - *Walking paths (1.28.0, opt-in from 1.28.2: `/nq paths on` first).* In Thousand Needles or Stonetalon with quests either side of a ridge or mesa: a few seconds after the route appears, the order should favour stops you can walk to directly, and the zone map's route line should bend round cliffs and lakes. `/nq paths` says whether this zone has data; `/nq paths off` goes back to straight lines. Watch for stutter when entering a zone with many quests: there should be none.
    - *Learning (1.27.4).* `/nq pace` on a fresh character says nothing is known yet. Do a kill quest: after 4 or 5 kills it should show seconds per kill and XP per kill close to what you saw. Do a drop quest: kills per quest item appears. Go afk for ten minutes mid-quest: the figure shouldn't jump. `/nq pace reset` clears it; `/nq pace off` returns the Route row to quest XP only.
    - *Pickups (1.27.3).* In a town with a quest on offer near you and a quest in your log that's handed in there: the guide should send you to the new quest before leaving town. Walk a route that passes a quest giver on the way out: it should be a stop on the way out, not after the turn-in. Watch the arrow over a few minutes of walking: it should never send you towards a stop, away, and back again without a quest changing.
    - *Order as you move (1.27.2).* With a quest ready to hand in to one side and another quest's objective and turn-in to the other, walk away from the first one: once the other order is clearly shorter, stop 1 should switch. Standing still or shuffling a few yards, it shouldn't flip. With the world map open, walk: the line's first leg should start at your arrow and move with you.
    - *Hand in before you leave (1.27.1).* Finish two or three quests handed in at different NPCs in a town or city, and have another quest out in the zone that's also handed in there. Walk into town: the guide should send you to all the ready turn-ins before pointing you out of town, never out and back. Then stand in the middle of an objective with a finished quest's NPC a short walk away: it should keep you on the objective.
    - *Too high.* Take a quest 5 or more levels above you: the guide should list it for later ("level 25, too high for now: on the route from level 22") and never point the arrow at it until you're within 3 levels. A finished one should still be handed in.
67. **Auto-accept only the quests the route will do (1.27.0).** Options > NeverQuest > Which quests: "Only ones the route will do". Talk to a quest giver with several quests: the ones the route would do (and class, profession, dungeon and attunement quests) should open and be accepted; a slow one far away should be left for you. An elite quest should be left while you're alone and taken in a group. Set it back to "Every quest": everything (not gray) is accepted again.
68. **The new 1-60 plans (1.27.0).** Guide tab > 1-60 plan: browse each race. Every leg should name a zone that fits its levels and your faction, with the legs running from 1 to 60 without gaps. Untick "Choose zones from the quest data" under Options > Guide & Routes and level a few levels: the guide should follow the plan's legs and not run out of quests in the zone it sends you to.

## Afterwards, send

- The `/nq check` text from step 1.

- Anything that looked wrong or felt clumsy, even if there was no error.

## Tips

- `/nq debug` prints what NeverQuest is doing (arrow target changes, route steps, automation). Turn it off the same way.
- If a feature hits repeated errors, NeverQuest pauses just that feature and tells you. `/reload` brings it back.
- `/nq reset` puts every panel back in its default spot.
